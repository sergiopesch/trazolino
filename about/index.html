Hi, I'm Sergio.

I'm a dad, and I wanted to connect with my daughter by drawing together with pen and paper. My drawing skills aren't great, so I built Trazolino. I was looking for an activity to enjoy with my daughter during half-term school breaks, and I think a lot of working parents are in the same boat.

Why I built Trazolino

I built the first version not too long ago, called it Little Line Studio, and got good feedback from working parents. So I decided to rename it to Trazolino, something shorter, memorable and friendly, and bought trazolino.org for it.

The main goal is guided drawing lessons on paper for kids. This is done with a grown-up right by their side. Right now it's available in nine languages, there's music, and there's instructions that are tailored in those languages, according to the culture, to some degree or extent.

My favourite is the Venezuelan Spanish voice, it helps my little one stay connected to her roots.

There's no ads, no accounts, no mic or camera, nothing gets uploaded. All the code is open source.

Next school break is all about Christmas, and I would love to make Trazolino Christmas themed. After that, handwriting and more pen and paper activities.
